# Lux Trademark Policy

This document describes how third parties may use Lux Industries Inc.'s trademarks. It does not grant any license. For specific permission, contact **legal@lux.network**.

## Marks

Lux Industries Inc. claims rights in (among others) the following marks:

- **Lux**
- **Lux Network**
- **Lux Industries**
- The Lux logo (geometric mark, all color variants).
- Any chain-specific brand marks for which Lux owns rights (chain logos, distinctive wordmarks).

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. Marks may be claimed under common-law rights, registered, or pending registration in one or more jurisdictions.

## Permitted Uses

The following uses generally do **not** require prior written permission, provided they comply with the conditions in the next section:

1. **Descriptive use.** Stating accurately that a product or service is built on, integrates with, or is compatible with Lux Network or another Lux technology. For example: "Wallet supports Lux Network", "Built on Lux", "Compatible with Lux Network".
2. **Journalistic and editorial use.** Use of the marks in news articles, blog posts, books, podcasts, videos, and other editorial content discussing Lux.
3. **Educational use.** Use in courses, tutorials, academic papers, and conference presentations discussing Lux technology.
4. **Nominative reference.** Use to identify Lux as the source of the underlying technology, without implying sponsorship or endorsement.

## Conditions

All permitted uses must:

1. **Be accurate.** Statements about Lux compatibility, performance, or features must be true.
2. **Not imply sponsorship or endorsement.** Do not state or imply that Lux Industries Inc. sponsors, endorses, or is otherwise affiliated with your product or service unless a separate written agreement is in place.
3. **Distinguish your brand.** Your product or service must have its own distinctive brand. Lux marks may be used to describe compatibility, not as the primary brand.
4. **Respect mark integrity.** Do not modify, animate, distort, or recolor the Lux logo. Use the official assets at `~/work/lux/brand/` (see [BRAND-USAGE-GUIDELINES.md](BRAND-USAGE-GUIDELINES.md)).
5. **Comply with brand guidelines.** Follow [BRAND-USAGE-GUIDELINES.md](BRAND-USAGE-GUIDELINES.md) for color, spacing, and contrast.

## Restricted Uses

The following uses **require** prior written permission from Lux Industries Inc.:

1. **Use in product or service names.** You may not name your product, service, company, organization, or domain after a Lux mark or in a way that creates likelihood of confusion. For example, "Lux Wallet", "LuxChain", "Lux DEX", "Lux Cloud" are restricted.
2. **Use in domain names** that incorporate a Lux mark in a way likely to cause confusion. Subdomains describing compatibility (for example, `lux-bridge.example.com` describing your bridge to Lux) may be acceptable; review them with Lux first.
3. **Use as a logo, icon, or app store identity** of your own product.
4. **Use suggesting partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement** of any kind, including "official", "certified", "approved", or similar terms.
5. **Use on physical merchandise** for sale.
6. **Use in connection with cryptocurrencies, tokens, or financial products** that are not issued or endorsed by Lux Industries Inc.

## Prohibited Uses

The following uses are **prohibited** and constitute infringement or dilution of Lux marks:

1. Use that is misleading, deceptive, or fraudulent.
2. Use in connection with illegal activities or content that violates applicable law.
3. Use in connection with products or services that infringe third-party intellectual-property rights.
4. Use that disparages Lux, its products, or its affiliates.
5. Use that creates a likelihood of confusion as to source, sponsorship, or affiliation.
6. Use intended to compete unfairly with Lux's own products or services.

## Enforcement

Lux Industries Inc. monitors trademark usage and may take action to protect its marks, including sending cease-and-desist communications, filing trademark oppositions, and pursuing litigation.

If you believe a third party is misusing Lux marks, please report it to **legal@lux.network** with details and supporting evidence.

## Requesting Permission

For uses outside the permitted scope, send a written request to **legal@lux.network** including:

- Your name, organization, and contact information.
- A description of the proposed use, including duration, geography, and channels.
- Mockups or samples of the proposed use.
- The marks you wish to use.

Lux will respond within a reasonable time. Permission, if granted, will be in writing and may be subject to conditions.
