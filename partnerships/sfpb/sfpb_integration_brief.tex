\begin{abstract}
\noindent This brief enumerates exactly what Lux Industries Inc.\ needs
from SF Private Bank (``SFPB'') to leverage the SFPB banking stack ---
SF Private Pay (PSP), multi-currency accounts and IBAN issuance, card
issuance, and merchant acquiring --- under the Lux \texttimes{} Local
Partner Strategic Partnership Agreement (50/50 net partnership revenue
above documented costs). It is the technical, commercial, and compliance
checklist for the joint kickoff. It is confidential between Lux
Industries and SFPB and is not for distribution. It is a working
document; it is not a contract and does not modify the underlying
partnership agreement.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textit{Reference:} OG-SP-2026-SFPB (partnership-agreement
reference). \textit{Audience:} SFPB technical + commercial leadership.
\textit{Purpose:} kickoff agenda + integration prerequisites.

\section{Context}

Lux Industries operates a native, regulator-neutral financial backend:
\texttt{bankd} ledger, \texttt{forexd} FX engine, \texttt{brokerd} smart
order router, \texttt{treasuryd} double-entry ledger + 14 leaf adapters,
\texttt{amld} AML/sanctions, \texttt{@luxfi/bridge} cross-chain MPC,
\texttt{lux/kms} HSM-backed key custody, \texttt{lux/mpc} threshold
signing. Customer surfaces sit on top: \texttt{lux.financial} (marketing
+ product), \texttt{app.lux.financial} (operational console), per-tenant
white-label deploys for downstream partners.

SFPB holds the US chartered-bank + BD + ATS + TA + FinCEN MSB +
state money-transmitter + CIRO + CSA + FINTRAC license stack and
operates SF Private Pay (Zeekash agent rails, MyATMen ATM network, card
issuance, ACH / wire / FedNow, stablecoin on/off-ramp).

The partnership is \textbf{50/50 net partnership revenue above
documented costs}, per the Lux \texttimes{} Local Partner Strategic
Partnership Agreement template already shared. This brief enumerates
exactly what we need from SFPB at the technical, commercial, and
compliance layers so we can wire the integration and start producing
partnership revenue.

\section{SF Private Pay (PSP)}

\paragraph{What SFPB gives us.}
A regulated US-side payment service provider product covering card
acceptance, alternative payment methods, agent cash rails (Zeekash),
ATM rails (MyATMen), card issuance, ACH / wire / FedNow, and stablecoin
on/off-ramp.

\subsection{Technical requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{API base URLs} --- production + sandbox.
  \item \textbf{API documentation} --- full OpenAPI / Swagger or
        equivalent spec covering every endpoint (auth, accounts,
        customers, payments, payouts, refunds, disputes, reports).
  \item \textbf{Auth model} --- OAuth client\_credentials, API key +
        HMAC, mTLS, or signed JWT. Token rotation policy and rate
        limits.
  \item \textbf{Webhook spec} --- per event type
        (\texttt{payment.authorized}, \texttt{payment.captured},
        \texttt{payment.failed}, \texttt{payout.completed},
        \texttt{dispute.opened}, \texttt{chargeback.received}, ...)
        with payload schema, HMAC signature header + algorithm, retry
        policy, idempotency-key header convention. We will subscribe at
        \texttt{https://api.lux.financial/webhooks/sfpay/<event\_type>}
        with HMAC verification before our callback handler accepts.
  \item \textbf{Idempotency-key convention} --- header name and how
        SFPB dedupes.
  \item \textbf{Test credentials} --- test credit card numbers, test
        bank routing/account numbers, sandbox failure scenarios
        (insufficient funds, sanctions hit, KYC mismatch, fraud
        reject, dispute).
  \item \textbf{Rate limits} --- per-key request/minute, burst, and
        tier.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Commercial requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Fee schedule} for each rail (per-transaction,
        percentage, monthly minimum).
  \item \textbf{Settlement mechanics} --- when funds clear; holdback /
        reserve requirements; FBO vs direct deposit.
  \item \textbf{Revenue-share} mechanics confirming compatibility with
        the 50/50 above documented costs structure in the partnership
        agreement.
  \item \textbf{Reporting feeds} --- daily reconciliation (CSV/JSON
        SFTP or webhook push), per-merchant breakdown, fee detail,
        dispute aging.
  \item \textbf{Chargeback / dispute liability} --- who carries the
        reserve, what's the partner-side process.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Compliance requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Shared responsibility matrix} explicitly stating who
        performs: KYC, KYB, OFAC sanctions screening, PEP screening,
        AML transaction monitoring, BSA SAR filing, CTR filing, FBAR,
        FATCA, Reg E disputes, Reg Z disclosures, PCI-DSS scope
        ownership.
  \item \textbf{PCI-DSS scope} --- does SFPay tokenise on the iframe /
        hosted fields so we stay out of scope, or do we need full
        SAQ D?
  \item \textbf{Card-network rules attestation} --- Visa CISP,
        Mastercard SDP, AMEX DSOP compliance evidence for SFPay's
        acquiring stack.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Lux integration target.}
\texttt{treasury/pkg/provider/sfpay/} --- new adapter implementing the
standard \texttt{Provider} interface (\texttt{CreateAccount},
\texttt{CreatePayment}, \texttt{GetPayment}, \texttt{ListPayments},
\texttt{GetBalance}, \texttt{GetFXQuote / CreateFXConversion} if
multi-currency, \texttt{CreateCounterparty}, \texttt{Capabilities}).
Env-gated on \texttt{SFPAY\_API\_KEY}. Webhook ingest at \texttt{forexd}
or \texttt{bankd} with HMAC verify.

\section{Multi-currency accounts + IBAN issuance}

\paragraph{What SFPB gives us.}
Account issuance across the SFPB regulated perimeter --- USD accounts
with ABA routing + account number (US); CAD accounts with transit +
account (Canada); GBP, EUR, and other currencies via partner EMI
relationships or correspondent banks; named or virtual IBANs where
the partner offers SEPA / Faster Payments access.

\subsection{Technical requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Account types} SFPB can issue: checking, savings,
        custodial (FBO), virtual sub-account, escrow.
  \item \textbf{Account-issuance API} --- create per-customer account,
        list, freeze, close. Per-customer KYC payload requirements.
  \item \textbf{Multi-currency support} --- which currencies natively,
        which via partner EMI, settlement mechanics for cross-currency
        transfers.
  \item \textbf{IBAN issuance} --- does SFPB issue named GBP / EUR
        IBANs directly or via a UK / EU EMI partner? If partner: who,
        what passport, shared-responsibility mapping. Or: do we
        provide our own EU EMI partner and use SFPB only for US/CA?
  \item \textbf{Sweep / cash-management} --- overnight sweep, balance
        interest if any, target-balance rules.
  \item \textbf{Statement + reporting} API --- daily balance feed,
        transaction detail, year-end 1099 / equivalent.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Commercial requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Per-account monthly fee} schedule.
  \item \textbf{Per-transaction fees} for each outbound rail at the
        account level (separate from PSP merchant acquiring).
  \item \textbf{Interest-bearing accounts} --- interest split if any.
  \item \textbf{Minimum balance / reserve} required to maintain
        account.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Compliance requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{CDD requirements} per customer type (US natural
        person, US legal entity, US trust, foreign natural person,
        foreign legal entity). Document checklist + acceptable ID
        types.
  \item \textbf{OFAC screening cadence} --- onboarding + per-transaction
        + ongoing.
  \item \textbf{CIP/BSA reliance} --- can SFPB rely on Simplici.io's
        KYC/CIP results under a Reliance Agreement (WRA), or does SFPB
        require independent CDD? Reliance Agreement template?
  \item \textbf{Reg E} dispute handling responsibility.
  \item \textbf{State money-transmitter} licensing footprint --- does
        SFPB cover all 50 states or do we route around unlicensed
        states?
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Lux integration target.}
\texttt{treasury/pkg/provider/sfpb-accounts/} --- implements
\texttt{CreateAccount}, \texttt{GetAccount}, \texttt{ListAccounts},
\texttt{GetBalance}, \texttt{CreateCounterparty} end-to-end; routes
outbound payment rails through SFPay (above) or directly where SFPB's
account API exposes them.

\section{Card issuance}

\paragraph{What SFPB gives us.}
BIN sponsorship via SFPB's Visa and/or Mastercard principal membership;
issuance of debit, prepaid, and (where applicable) credit cards under
our brand or partner-tenant brand, physical and virtual.

\subsection{Technical requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Card-issuing platform} --- is SFPB on Marqeta, Lithic,
        Galileo, FIS, i2c, in-house? Whatever it is, we need API
        access (read-only at minimum; ideally full create / freeze /
        replace / cancel).
  \item \textbf{Card product configurations} --- debit, prepaid
        (open-loop, closed-loop), credit, virtual-only, single-use,
        expense-controlled.
  \item \textbf{Funding model} --- direct from cardholder account,
        FBO pooled, ledger-backed-on-our-side with daily settlement
        to SFPB.
  \item \textbf{Auth stream} --- real-time authorization webhook
        (sub-200ms decision) with our risk engine (\texttt{amld} +
        custom rules) participating in the auth decision. Spec for
        the auth-request payload + decision response.
  \item \textbf{3DS} --- 3DS2 challenge integration, ACS provider,
        fall-back path.
  \item \textbf{Card-network tokenisation} --- Visa Token Service /
        Mastercard MDES support for Apple Pay / Google Pay /
        Samsung Pay.
  \item \textbf{Physical card production} --- vendor (Perfect Plastic /
        CPI / G+D M\"uhlbauer / Tag Systems / Idemia), turnaround,
        custom artwork approval flow, EMV chip personalisation,
        contactless.
  \item \textbf{PIN management} --- PIN issuance, change, unblock;
        PIN-Verify service.
  \item \textbf{Dispute / chargeback} --- flow into the network
        dispute API; SFPB-side process for representment +
        arbitration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Commercial requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Setup fees} --- per BIN, per card product, artwork
        approval.
  \item \textbf{Per-card fees} --- issuance, monthly active,
        transaction (network + processing), 3DS, tokenisation,
        physical-card production.
  \item \textbf{Interchange revenue share} --- the headline economic.
        Visa/MC posted interchange, less network assessments, less
        processing fees, less SFPB take; net interchange flows into
        the 50/50 partnership pool.
  \item \textbf{Cardholder funding} --- is there a customer-deposit
        reserve requirement?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Compliance requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{KYC for cardholders} --- CIP requirements; SFPB-required
        or our WRA-reliance.
  \item \textbf{CARD Act / Reg Z} disclosure ownership for credit /
        prepaid.
  \item \textbf{Reg E} dispute ownership.
  \item \textbf{OFAC} screening on cardholder onboarding + ongoing.
  \item \textbf{Network rules} --- Visa Operating Regulations,
        Mastercard Rules attestation; PCI-DSS compliance scope for
        cardholder data on our side.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Lux integration target.}
\texttt{treasury/pkg/provider/sfpb-cards/} implements the card-issuance
side of the \texttt{Provider} interface (\texttt{CreateCard},
\texttt{GetCard}, \texttt{FreezeCard}, \texttt{ReplaceCard},
\texttt{ListTransactionsForCard}). Auth-stream webhook handled by
\texttt{bankd} with \texttt{amld} risk-gate participation. Card-decline
reasons emitted to Lux Network as content-addressed audit events.

\section{Merchant acquiring (PSP card acceptance for our partners)}

\paragraph{What SFPB gives us.}
Acquiring services for our downstream merchant partners --- onboarding,
MID provisioning, settlement, dispute management --- across the
jurisdictions where SFPB or its acquiring partner holds the requisite
acquirer licences.

\subsection{Requirements}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Acquirer jurisdictions} --- which Visa / Mastercard
        regions, plus domestic schemes (AMEX, Discover, Diners, JCB,
        UnionPay, Interac).
  \item \textbf{Onboarding API} --- KYB submission, beneficial-ownership
        attestation, MCC assignment, monthly volume cap.
  \item \textbf{Underwriting timeline} --- SLA from KYB submitted to
        MID issued.
  \item \textbf{MID provisioning} --- per merchant, per MCC; descriptor
        rules, soft-descriptor support, dynamic-descriptor by
        transaction.
  \item \textbf{Settlement schedule} --- T+N per merchant tier, daily
        ACH or wire, holdback / reserve mechanics.
  \item \textbf{Dispute API} --- chargeback receive, representment
        submit, evidence upload, arbitration escalation.
  \item \textbf{Reporting} --- per-merchant daily + monthly fee
        detail, gross + net + interchange + assessments + processing.
\end{itemize}

(Compliance posture inherits from \S2 SFPay.)

\paragraph{Lux integration target.}
The same \texttt{treasury/pkg/provider/sfpay/} adapter handles the
merchant-acquiring surface --- same auth, same webhooks, separate
endpoint family.

\section{Cross-cutting operational + commercial requirements}

\subsection{Operational}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Sandbox vs production parity} --- every endpoint above
        must work in sandbox with realistic data, before production
        credentials issue.
  \item \textbf{API status page} + status webhook.
  \item \textbf{Maintenance windows} --- schedule, advance notice,
        runbook for failover or graceful-degradation.
  \item \textbf{On-call escalation path} --- named technical liaison,
        on-call rotation contact, SLA on critical incidents
        (P0 / P1 / P2 / P3 with response + resolution targets).
  \item \textbf{Production cutover plan} --- phased rollout
        (shadow $\to$ 1\% $\to$ 10\% $\to$ 100\%), per-product
        enablement, named go/no-go gates.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Commercial}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Documented Costs definition} alignment with the
        partnership agreement (which SFPB-incurred costs count as
        Documented; e.g.\ network interchange, processing, sponsor
        fees yes; SFPB overhead / marketing / cost of capital no).
  \item \textbf{Net Partnership Revenue reconciliation cadence} ---
        monthly statements within 15 business days of month-end,
        joint reconciliation meeting within 5 business days of
        statement delivery, net wire within 30 days.
  \item \textbf{Audit rights} --- biannual, on 30 days' notice, at
        audited party's expense if >5\% underpayment.
  \item \textbf{Customer markup retention} --- SFPB confirms 100\%
        retention of any customer-facing markups by either party
        stays with the marking-up party (the standard from the
        partnership agreement).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Compliance shared-responsibility matrix}
Annex A to the partnership agreement should formalise the SFPB-side vs
Lux-side regulated-function allocation across:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item BSA / AML programme ownership
  \item OFAC sanctions screening
  \item CIP / CDD / EDD
  \item Suspicious Activity Reports (FinCEN)
  \item Currency Transaction Reports (FinCEN)
  \item FBAR / FATCA / CRS / GoAML
  \item Reg E (electronic fund transfers) dispute resolution
  \item Reg Z (truth in lending) disclosures
  \item Reg D (regulation D, account-class restrictions if any)
  \item Network operating-rules compliance (Visa CISP, MC SDP)
  \item PCI-DSS scope ownership per integration surface
  \item Card Act disclosures (where applicable)
  \item State money-transmitter licensing footprint
  \item CIRO / CSA Canadian rules
  \item FCA / FINTRAC / EBA cross-border rules where SFPB extends
\end{itemize}

\section{What we will give SFPB in return}

For reference, the symmetric obligations from the partnership agreement
that satisfy SFPB's side of the deal:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Simplici.io} white-labelled onboarding, KYC, AML,
        sanctions, PEP with the per-check pass-through pricing in
        the agreement; SFPB can rely on Simplici under a WRA.
  \item \textbf{Lux Network} content-addressed audit substrate
        covering every state transition relevant to SFPB-supervised
        activity, with cryptographic commitments retainable for the
        durations required by CBS, FinCEN, OFAC, FATCA.
  \item \textbf{Identity} --- every customer onboarded once, used
        across every product surface (account, card, FX, crypto) with
        consistent reference IDs.
  \item \textbf{Distribution} --- our customer base served through
        SFPB-issued accounts, SFPB-issued cards, SFPB-acquired
        merchants. Concrete volume forecast in the partnership-agreement
        schedule.
  \item \textbf{Net Partnership Revenue} monthly statements and 50/50
        settlement on the agreed cadence.
  \item \textbf{Operational integration} of our risk and AML rules
        into the SFPB auth stream so SFPB sees consistent risk-gate
        decisions.
\end{itemize}

\section{Decision checklist for the joint kickoff call}

To convert this into a green-lit project we propose the following items
as agenda for the kickoff:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item SFPB confirms API documentation will be shared under NDA within
        5 business days.
  \item SFPB confirms a sandbox tenant will be provisioned for Lux
        engineering within 10 business days.
  \item SFPB confirms the named technical liaison + on-call escalation
        path.
  \item SFPB confirms the shared-responsibility matrix (Annex A above)
        is acceptable in principle and identifies any function it would
        prefer to retain or delegate.
  \item SFPB confirms its UK/EU IBAN partner (if any) and the
        operational model for cross-currency settlement.
  \item SFPB confirms the card BIN sponsor / issuing platform and any
        prior commercial dependencies (e.g.\ existing Marqeta or Lithic
        relationship) that we should integrate to vs around.
  \item Joint sign-off on the partnership-agreement Annex A
        (shared-responsibility) and Annex B (Documented Costs
        definition) within 15 business days.
\end{enumerate}
