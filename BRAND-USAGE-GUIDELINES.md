# Lux Brand Usage Guidelines

These guidelines describe how to use Lux Industries Inc.'s brand assets: logos, names, color, and typography. They complement [TRADEMARK-POLICY.md](TRADEMARK-POLICY.md), which governs the legal scope of permitted use.

## Asset Source

Authoritative brand assets are maintained at `~/work/lux/brand/`. The repository contains:

- Logo files (SVG, PNG at multiple resolutions).
- Color palette specifications (`brand.json`).
- Typography references.
- Demo and reference HTML.

Always use the official assets from the brand repository. Do not recreate logos by hand, attempt to vectorize bitmap copies, or substitute similar fonts without confirming.

## Logo

### Permitted

- Display the Lux logo to indicate compatibility, integration, or accurate descriptive reference, subject to the conditions below and the [Trademark Policy](TRADEMARK-POLICY.md).
- Use the logo in editorial, journalistic, and educational content discussing Lux.
- Use the logo at sufficient size to remain legible.

### Prohibited

- Modifying, animating, distorting, recoloring, or adding effects to the logo.
- Using the logo as the primary brand identity of a non-Lux product.
- Combining the logo with another logo in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or partnership without prior written agreement.
- Reproducing the logo at sizes too small to be legible.
- Placing the logo on a background that compromises legibility or brand integrity.

### Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the logo's smallest distinct shape. Do not crowd the logo with other graphic elements.

### Minimum Size

The Lux logo should not be reproduced smaller than 24 pixels tall in digital contexts or 0.25 inches tall in print, except in favicons and other contexts where smaller sizes are technically required.

## Name Usage

- The company is **Lux Industries Inc.** (full legal name) or **Lux Industries** (common reference).
- The network and ecosystem brand is **Lux** or **Lux Network**.
- Use sentence-style capitalization in prose ("Lux"), not all-caps ("LUX") except where required by typographic context.
- Do not abbreviate "Lux Industries Inc." as "LII" or any other initialism in prose.
- The plural is "Lux Network products"; do not pluralize the brand mark itself.

## Color Palette

The authoritative palette is defined in `~/work/lux/brand/brand.json`. Do not invent new palette colors and present them as Lux brand colors. When integrating Lux brand colors into a third-party design, use the values from the JSON file directly.

## Typography

- Lux's primary typeface, secondary typeface, and monospace typeface are documented in `~/work/lux/brand/`. Confirm and follow.
- Do not substitute look-alike fonts in marketing materials presented as Lux content.
- For technical documentation referencing Lux, no specific Lux typography is required; use what fits your context.

## Co-Branding

Co-branding (placing the Lux logo alongside another organization's logo on materials presented as joint work) requires prior written agreement with Lux Industries Inc. Contact **legal@lux.network**.

## Merchandise

Use of Lux marks on merchandise for sale (T-shirts, stickers, hats, etc.) requires prior written permission from Lux Industries Inc.

Use of Lux marks on merchandise for non-commercial distribution (community swag, conference giveaways) is generally permitted for community groups in good standing, subject to compliance with these guidelines and the [Trademark Policy](TRADEMARK-POLICY.md).

## Examples

### Acceptable

- A wallet vendor's website states "Supports Lux Network" with the Lux logo at small size near a list of supported networks.
- A conference talk's title slide reads "Building on Lux: Lessons from Production".
- A technical blog post titled "How Lux's consensus differs from PBFT" with reference to the Lux logo in the header.

### Not Acceptable

- A wallet vendor names its product "LuxWallet" without permission.
- A token issuer brands its token "Official Lux Token" without authorization.
- A company places the Lux logo on its homepage in a "Trusted Partners" section without an actual partnership.

## Reporting Misuse

Report suspected misuse of Lux brand assets to **legal@lux.network** with supporting details and evidence.

## Updates

Brand guidelines may evolve. The current version in this repository is canonical for legal-policy purposes; the design specifications in `~/work/lux/brand/` are canonical for asset integrity.
