- **Account information.** Name, email address, organization, password (hashed; never stored in plain text), and other information you provide when creating an account or contacting us.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data.** Pages visited, features used, and actions taken in the Service.
- **Device and connection data.** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and time-zone settings.
- **Cookies and similar technologies.** As described in Section 6.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Authentication.** When you sign in via a third-party identity provider, that provider may share profile information with us as authorized by you.
- **Analytics and infrastructure providers.** Aggregated usage information and operational metrics.

3. Communicate with you about the Service, including security and operational notices.
4. Bill for paid features and process payments.
5. Detect, prevent, and respond to security incidents and abuse.
6. Comply with legal obligations.
7. Improve the Service through analytics on aggregated, de-identified data.

## 5. How We Share Information

We share information only as follows:

1. **Service providers.** With vendors who process information on our behalf, such as cloud infrastructure, payment processors, analytics providers, and customer-support tooling, under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
2. **Legal compliance.** Where required by applicable law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lux, our users, or others.
3. **Business transfers.** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality and continuity-of-policy obligations.
4. **With your consent.** Where you direct us to share information with a specific third party.

We do not share personal information with advertisers for cross-context behavioral advertising.
