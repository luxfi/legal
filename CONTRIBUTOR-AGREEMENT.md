# Contributor License Agreement (Template)

This Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") governs intellectual-property contributions from outside contributors to repositories owned by **Lux Industries Inc.** ("Lux"). This CLA applies to contributions to public Lux repositories (Tier 1 BSD-3 and Tier 2 Eco). Closed Tier 3 repositories accept contributions only under separate work-for-hire agreement.

This is a **template**. The version executed for a specific contributor must be filled in and counsel-reviewed.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"You"** means the individual or entity submitting a Contribution.

1.2 **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any modification or addition to existing work, that You intentionally submit to a Lux repository through any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication, including pull requests, patches, and issue comments containing code.

1.3 **"Receiving Repository"** means the Lux repository to which You submit a Contribution, identified by its URL on `github.com/luxfi/*`.

1.4 **"Receiving License"** means the license under which the Receiving Repository is distributed, as recorded in the repository's `LICENSE` file at the time of contribution (typically BSD-3-Clause for Tier 1 or the Lux Eco License for Tier 2).

## 2. Grant of Copyright License

2.1 You grant Lux and recipients of software distributed by Lux a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works under the Receiving License and any later version of that license.

2.2 Lux may also distribute Your Contribution under additional licenses, including for commercial licensing as described in [LICENSING-POLICY.md](LICENSING-POLICY.md), provided that the public version of the Receiving Repository remains available under the Receiving License.

## 3. Grant of Patent License

3.1 You grant Lux and recipients of software distributed by Lux a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this Section) patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Your Contribution, where such license applies only to those patent claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by combination of Your Contribution with the Receiving Repository.

3.2 If any entity initiates patent litigation against You or any other entity (including a cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit) alleging that Your Contribution, or the Receiving Repository to which You contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licenses granted to that entity under this CLA for that Contribution or Receiving Repository terminate as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 4. Substantial Contributions and IP Assignment

4.1 For Contributions that are **substantial** (defined below), You also assign to Lux Industries Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to the Contribution, to the extent permitted by law, in addition to (and not in lieu of) the licenses granted in Sections 2 and 3.

4.2 A Contribution is **substantial** if it (a) introduces a new module, package, or service; (b) implements a published Lux specification; (c) exceeds 500 lines of code in a single pull request; or (d) is identified by Lux as substantial at the time of acceptance.

4.3 If assignment is not permitted by Your jurisdiction, You instead grant Lux the broadest license permitted by Your jurisdiction, on the terms in Sections 2 and 3, to achieve substantively the same result.

5.3 **No Conflicting Obligations.** Submission of Your Contributions does not violate any agreement with a third party.

5.4 **Notification.** You shall notify Lux promptly if any of the representations above becomes inaccurate.
