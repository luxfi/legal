# Employee Invention and IP Assignment Agreement (Template)

This Employee Invention and IP Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") governs the assignment of intellectual property created by employees and contractors of **Lux Industries Inc.** ("Company") in the course of their engagement.

This is a **template**. The version executed for a specific employee or contractor must be filled in and counsel-reviewed.

---

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is between **Lux Industries Inc.** ("Company") and `[EMPLOYEE / CONTRACTOR NAME]` ("Employee"), and is effective as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` (the "Effective Date").

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **"Engagement"** means Employee's employment or contractor relationship with Company.

2.2 **"Inventions"** means all discoveries, developments, designs, improvements, inventions, formulas, software programs, processes, techniques, know-how, and data, whether patentable or registerable under copyright or similar laws.

2.3 **"Company Inventions"** means Inventions that Employee, alone or with others, conceives, develops, or reduces to practice during the Engagement that (a) relate to Company's business, research, or development, (b) result from work performed for Company, or (c) are made using Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, trade secrets, or time.

2.4 **"Background IP"** means Inventions Employee owned, developed, or had rights in prior to the Effective Date and that are listed in **Schedule BG**.

## 3. Assignment of Company Inventions

3.1 Employee assigns to Company, irrevocably and without further consideration, all right, title, and interest in and to all Company Inventions, including all associated intellectual-property rights worldwide.

3.2 Employee shall promptly disclose to Company in writing all Company Inventions.

3.3 Employee shall execute all documents and take all actions reasonably requested by Company, at Company's expense, to perfect Company's rights in Company Inventions, including the execution of patent applications, copyright registrations, and confirmatory assignments.

3.4 If Employee fails to execute any document required under Section 3.3 within a reasonable time, Employee irrevocably appoints Company as Employee's attorney-in-fact to execute such documents on Employee's behalf, solely for the purpose of perfecting Company's rights in Company Inventions.

## 4. Statutory Carve-Out

4.1 The assignment in Section 3.1 does not apply to any Invention that qualifies fully for an applicable statutory exclusion, including (where applicable) inventions developed entirely on Employee's own time without using Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information, except for inventions that (a) relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice to Company's business or actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development of Company, or (b) result from any work performed by Employee for Company.

## 5. Background IP

5.1 Employee shall list all Background IP in **Schedule BG**. If no Background IP is listed, Employee represents that Employee has no Background IP that is or will be incorporated into any Company Invention.

5.2 If Employee incorporates any Background IP into a Company Invention, Employee grants Company a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable license to use, reproduce, modify, and distribute that Background IP as part of or in connection with the Company Invention.

## 6. Substantial Contributions to Company IP

6.1 To the extent Employee makes substantial contributions to existing Company intellectual property using Background IP not listed in Schedule BG or third-party material, Employee shall (a) identify those contributions in writing to Company, (b) identify the source and license terms of any third-party material, and (c) cooperate in good faith to ensure Company has the necessary rights to use the resulting Company Invention.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Employee shall hold all Company Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not use, disclose, or reproduce it except as necessary to perform Employee's duties for Company.

7.2 Employee's confidentiality obligations survive termination of the Engagement indefinitely with respect to trade secrets and for `[CONFIDENTIALITY TAIL TBD; suggest 3 years]` with respect to other Confidential Information.

## 8. Return of Materials

On termination of the Engagement, Employee shall return to Company all Company materials in Employee's possession or control, including documents, equipment, devices, source code, credentials, and any copies, and shall not retain copies in any form.

## 9. No Conflicts

9.1 Employee represents that entering into this Agreement does not breach any other agreement to which Employee is a party.

9.2 During the Engagement, Employee shall not engage in any outside activity that conflicts with Employee's duties to Company without Company's prior written consent.

## 10. Independent Contractor (if applicable)

10.1 If Employee is engaged as an independent contractor rather than an employee, the parties acknowledge that Employee is not an employee of Company and is not entitled to employee benefits. Employee is responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under the Engagement.

10.2 In an independent-contractor relationship, the assignment of Company Inventions in Section 3 functions as a work-for-hire arrangement to the maximum extent permitted by law, with explicit assignment as a backstop where work-for-hire treatment does not apply.

## 11. General

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of `[GOVERNING LAW JURISDICTION]`. For employees, this should typically be the law of the state in which Employee primarily works.

11.2 **At-Will Employment.** Nothing in this Agreement alters the at-will nature of Employee's employment, where applicable.

11.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with any offer letter and any separate confidentiality, non-solicitation, or non-competition agreement executed by Employee, is the entire agreement between the parties on the subject matter.

11.4 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the unenforceable provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to render it enforceable.

11.6 **Assignment.** Company may assign this Agreement to any successor without Employee's consent. Employee may not assign this Agreement.

11.7 **Survival.** Sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 11 survive termination of the Engagement.
