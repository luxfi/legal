# Corporate Contributor License Agreement (Template)

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Corporate CLA") governs intellectual-property contributions from an organization and its designated personnel to repositories owned by **Lux Industries Inc.** ("Lux"). It is the entity-level counterpart to the individual [CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md) ("Individual CLA") and applies to contributions to public Lux repositories (Tier 1 BSD-3 and Tier 2 Eco). Closed Tier 3 repositories accept contributions only under a separate work-for-hire agreement.

This Corporate CLA does not replace the Individual CLA; each Designated Contributor must also accept the Individual CLA in the ordinary course of submitting Contributions (for example, by opening a pull request). This Corporate CLA binds the Company in addition.

This is a **template**. The version executed for a specific Company must be filled in and counsel-reviewed.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Company"** means the entity identified in the signature block below, including its wholly-owned subsidiaries that adopt this Corporate CLA in writing.

1.2 **"Designated Contributor"** means an employee or contractor of the Company listed in **Schedule A** (Designated Employees), as updated from time to time in accordance with Section 7.

1.3 **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any modification or addition to existing work, that a Designated Contributor intentionally submits to a Lux repository on behalf of the Company through any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication, including pull requests, patches, and issue comments containing code.

1.4 **"Receiving Repository"** means the Lux repository to which a Contribution is submitted, identified by its URL on `github.com/luxfi/*`.

1.5 **"Receiving License"** means the license under which the Receiving Repository is distributed, as recorded in the repository's `LICENSE` file at the time of contribution (typically BSD-3-Clause for Tier 1 or the Lux Eco License for Tier 2).

1.6 Terms not defined in this Corporate CLA have the meanings given in the Individual CLA.

## 2. Grant of Copyright License

2.1 The Company grants Lux and recipients of software distributed by Lux a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Contributions made by Designated Contributors and such derivative works under the Receiving License and any later version of that license.

2.2 Lux may also distribute Contributions under additional licenses, including for commercial licensing as described in [LICENSING-POLICY.md](LICENSING-POLICY.md), provided that the public version of the Receiving Repository remains available under the Receiving License.

## 3. Grant of Patent License

3.1 The Company grants Lux and recipients of software distributed by Lux a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this Section) patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Contributions made by Designated Contributors, where such license applies only to those patent claims licensable by the Company that are necessarily infringed by such Contributions alone or by combination of such Contributions with the Receiving Repository.

3.2 If the Company or any of its affiliates initiates patent litigation against Lux or any other entity (including a cross-claim or counterclaim in a lawsuit) alleging that a Contribution, or the Receiving Repository to which a Designated Contributor contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licenses granted to the Company under this Corporate CLA for that Contribution or Receiving Repository terminate as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 4. Substantial Contributions and IP Assignment

4.1 For Contributions that are **substantial** (defined in Section 4.2 of the Individual CLA and incorporated here by reference), the Company assigns to Lux Industries Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to the Contribution, to the extent permitted by law, in addition to (and not in lieu of) the licenses granted in Sections 2 and 3.

4.2 If assignment is not permitted by the Company's jurisdiction, the Company instead grants Lux the broadest license permitted by that jurisdiction, on the terms in Sections 2 and 3, to achieve substantively the same result.

## 5. Representations

The Company represents that:

5.1 **Authority.** The signatory listed below is duly authorized to bind the Company to this Corporate CLA, and the Company has the right to license and assign the Contributions of Designated Contributors as set forth herein.

5.2 **Employment / Engagement.** Each Designated Contributor is an employee or contractor of the Company at the time of Contribution, and the Company has all rights necessary in such Designated Contributor's work product (whether by employment contract, contractor agreement, work-for-hire doctrine, or statutory assignment) to grant the licenses and assignments in Sections 2, 3, and 4.

5.3 **Originality.** Each Contribution by a Designated Contributor is, to the Company's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, the original creation of the Designated Contributor and the Company, or any non-original portions and their associated license terms are identified in the Contribution.

5.4 **No Conflicting Obligations.** Submission of Contributions under this Corporate CLA does not violate any agreement between the Company and a third party.

5.5 **Notification.** The Company shall notify Lux promptly if any of the representations above becomes inaccurate, or if a Designated Contributor ceases to be authorized to make Contributions on behalf of the Company (see Section 7).

## 6. Disclaimer

The Company provides Contributions on an "AS IS" basis, without warranties of any kind, either express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement, except as expressly set forth in Section 5.

## 7. Schedule A Maintenance

7.1 The Company may add or remove Designated Contributors by delivering to Lux an updated **Schedule A** signed by an authorized signatory of the Company. An update is effective on the date Lux acknowledges receipt by email to the contact listed below.

7.2 Removal of a Designated Contributor from Schedule A is prospective only. Contributions made before removal remain governed by this Corporate CLA.

7.3 The Company shall not knowingly permit any person who is not a Designated Contributor to submit Contributions on behalf of the Company.

## 8. No Obligation

Lux is not obligated to use, distribute, or include any Contribution. Acceptance of a Contribution is at Lux's sole discretion.

## 9. Miscellaneous

9.1 **Relationship to Individual CLA.** This Corporate CLA is in addition to, and does not replace, the Individual CLA, which each Designated Contributor accepts in the ordinary course of submitting Contributions. In the event of conflict between this Corporate CLA and the Individual CLA with respect to the Company's rights and obligations, this Corporate CLA controls.

9.2 **Governing Law.** This Corporate CLA is governed by the laws of `[GOVERNING LAW JURISDICTION; suggest Delaware]`, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

9.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Corporate CLA, together with the Individual CLA accepted by each Designated Contributor, is the entire agreement between the Company and Lux regarding the subject matter and supersedes any prior Corporate CLA executed by the Company.

9.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

9.5 **Counterparts.** This Corporate CLA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and which together constitute one agreement.
